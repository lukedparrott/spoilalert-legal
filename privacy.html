Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 24, 2026 • App: SpoilAlert

Your privacy is fundamental to our mission. SpoilAlert is designed from the ground up to respect your data ownership and minimize data collection. We do not track you across apps, sell your data, or use advertising identifiers.

1. Data Stored on Your Device (Local Storage)

All pantry and grocery inventory items, shelf-life dates, consumption logs, and settings are stored exclusively on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. We do not operate external database servers containing your personal kitchen records.

2. Camera & OCR Processing

SpoilAlert requests camera access solely to:

  • Scan grocery barcodes (EAN-8, EAN-13, UPC-E) using Apple's native VisionKit.
  • Scan printed package expiration dates and grocery receipts using Apple's on-device Vision text recognition engine.

All camera analysis takes place entirely in real time on your iPhone. Video feeds, camera frames, and packaging images are never saved to external servers or transmitted off your device.

3. Third-Party Service Queries

  • Open Food Facts API: When you scan a product barcode, the app sends only the anonymous numerical barcode string to the public Open Food Facts API (world.openfoodfacts.org) to retrieve product descriptions, categories, and shelf-life suggestions. No personal identity, user account, or device identifier is attached to these queries.
  • Apple StoreKit: If you purchase a SpoilAlert Pro subscription, payment processing and receipt verification are handled directly by Apple Inc. through StoreKit 2. We do not receive or store your credit card or financial details.

4. Push & Local Notifications

SpoilAlert schedules freshness alerts locally on your device at 9:00 AM (3 days prior, 1 day prior, and day of expiration). These notifications are generated locally via iOS UserNotifications without requiring a remote notification server.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. The application is suitable for general audiences.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
