Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does barcode and receipt scanning work?

Open the scanner tab and aim your camera at any retail food barcode (EAN or UPC) or printed grocery paper receipt. SpoilAlert uses Apple's VisionKit directly on your device to parse items and suggest optimal shelf-life dates.

How do I scan printed expiration dates on packages?

Tap "Scan Expiration Date" on the item sheet to activate our OCR text scanner. Align the camera with printed date stamps (e.g., "EXP 11/24/2026" or "BEST BY 14 NOV") and the date will be parsed automatically.

What is included in the Free tier vs. Pro?

The Free tier tracks up to 5 active groceries with full manual entry and 9:00 AM freshness alerts. SpoilAlert Pro unlocks unlimited inventory, automated barcode and paper receipt OCR scanning, and zero-waste streaks.

How do I restore my Pro subscription?

Open the Paywall or Settings in the app, scroll down, and tap Restore Apple ID Purchases. Apple StoreKit will verify your entitlement instantly.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed directly by Apple. On your iPhone, open the Settings app > tap your Name / Apple ID > tap Subscriptions > select SpoilAlert > tap Cancel Subscription.
